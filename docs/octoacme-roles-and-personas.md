# OctoAcme Personas

This document defines typical roles and responsibilities used in OctoAcme project docs and exercises.

---

## Developers

### Role Summary
Developers design, build, test, and deliver software components. They collaborate with product and project leads to implement features that meet acceptance criteria and quality standards.

### Responsibilities
- Implement features and fixes to meet acceptance criteria
- Write and maintain tests and documentation
- Participate in design and code reviews
- Assist in estimating and planning work
- Help identify technical risks and propose mitigations

### Goals
- Deliver reliable, maintainable code
- Reduce cycle time from idea to production
- Maintain high test coverage and observability

### Typical Communication
- Daily standups and sprint planning
- PR descriptions and code review comments
- Technical design docs when needed

---

## Product Managers

### Role Summary
Product Managers define what should be built to deliver customer and business value. They own the product vision, prioritize the backlog, and measure outcomes.

### Responsibilities
- Define problem statements and success metrics
- Prioritize the roadmap and backlog
- Collaborate with stakeholders and engineering on trade-offs
- Validate solutions through user research and metrics

### Goals
- Maximize customer value and impact
- Make clear, data-driven prioritization decisions
- Ensure product-market fit and usability

### Typical Communication
- Weekly alignment with PM and engineering leads
- Roadmap updates and stakeholder briefings
- Acceptance criteria and feature specs

---

## Project Managers

### Role Summary
Project Managers coordinate delivery activities, manage schedules, risks, and communications. They enable the team to deliver on commitments efficiently.

### Responsibilities
- Create and maintain project plans and timelines
- Manage risks, dependencies, and resource constraints
- Facilitate meetings (kickoff, planning, retrospectives)
- Ensure consistent project documentation and status reporting
- Coordinate cross-team and stakeholder communication

### Goals
- Deliver projects on time and within scope
- Minimize unplanned work and escalations
- Maintain transparency and alignment across stakeholders

### Typical Communication
- Weekly status updates and stakeholder reports
- Risk registers and decision logs
- Coordination via project boards and meeting facilitation

---

## Scrum Masters / Delivery Leads

### Role Summary
Scrum Masters or Delivery Leads help the team maintain an effective delivery cadence, remove impediments, and continuously improve how work is delivered. The role facilitates the process but does not replace the Project Manager's overall coordination responsibilities or the Product Manager's prioritization decisions.

### Responsibilities
- Facilitate team ceremonies and keep them focused on their intended outcomes
- Help Developers identify and resolve team-level blockers, escalating cross-team dependencies
- Coach the team on agreed delivery practices and continuous improvement
- Surface delivery risks and capacity concerns early

### Goals
- Enable predictable, sustainable team delivery
- Reduce the impact of impediments and ineffective process
- Help the team improve its ways of working

### Typical Communication
- Coordinate delivery cadence and dependencies with Project Managers
- Work with Product Managers to ensure backlog items are ready for planning
- Facilitate planning, standups, reviews, and retrospectives with Developers

---

## Release Managers / Release Coordinators

### Role Summary
Release Managers or Release Coordinators organize release readiness and deployment activities. They coordinate the release process and make readiness and risk visible; technical approvals remain with the designated engineering, quality, and security owners.

### Responsibilities
- Coordinate release schedules, readiness reviews, and deployment checklists
- Confirm that required testing, release notes, and rollback or mitigation plans are prepared
- Track release blockers and communicate status and decisions
- Coordinate post-deployment verification and release communications

### Goals
- Deliver releases in a controlled, well-communicated way
- Reduce deployment risk and improve recovery readiness
- Ensure relevant teams know their release responsibilities

### Typical Communication
- Coordinate build and deployment readiness with Developers
- Confirm test completion and open quality risks with QA Leads
- Align release timing and dependencies with Project Managers
- Coordinate stakeholder announcements and support readiness

---

## QA Leads / Test Managers

### Role Summary
QA Leads or Test Managers coordinate the quality strategy and testing effort, ensuring risks and validation status are visible. They partner with the team on quality and do not replace Developers' responsibility to build and test their work.

### Responsibilities
- Define test approaches and coordinate appropriate test coverage
- Align test plans with acceptance criteria and critical user journeys
- Report quality risks, defects, and readiness gaps
- Coordinate integration, regression, and release validation where applicable

### Goals
- Detect important defects and quality risks early
- Provide clear evidence of product readiness against acceptance criteria
- Make testing responsibilities and outcomes transparent

### Typical Communication
- Partner with Product Managers to clarify acceptance criteria
- Collaborate with Developers on testability, defect triage, and fixes
- Coordinate quality gates and release risks with Project and Release Managers

---

## UX / UI Designers

### Role Summary
UX / UI Designers shape usable and accessible product experiences, translating user needs into flows, interaction patterns, and visual designs.

### Responsibilities
- Research user needs and map key journeys and workflows
- Create and validate interaction designs, prototypes, and interface specifications
- Apply usability and accessibility considerations throughout design
- Share design rationale and identify experience risks or trade-offs

### Goals
- Deliver understandable, usable, and accessible experiences
- Keep product decisions grounded in user needs
- Reduce implementation rework through clear design collaboration

### Typical Communication
- Work with Product Managers to connect user needs to product outcomes and priorities
- Collaborate with Developers on feasibility, implementation details, and design handoff
- Partner with QA Leads on usability and accessibility validation
- Share research findings and design decisions with stakeholders

---

## Business Analysts

### Role Summary
Business Analysts clarify business needs, processes, rules, and requirements so the team can make informed scope and implementation decisions.

### Responsibilities
- Elicit and document stakeholder needs, business rules, and process flows
- Identify assumptions, dependencies, and gaps in requirements
- Support clear, testable requirements and acceptance criteria
- Trace requirements to planned work and validation where appropriate

### Goals
- Create shared understanding of the problem and expected outcomes
- Reduce ambiguity and late changes caused by misunderstood requirements
- Help teams make scope and dependency decisions using clear information

### Typical Communication
- Partner with Product Managers on outcomes, scope, and backlog clarity
- Work with Developers and QA Leads to clarify rules and confirm feasibility and testability
- Coordinate scope, dependencies, and requirement decisions with Project Managers
- Facilitate requirement discussions with stakeholders

---

## Security / Compliance Leads

### Role Summary
Security or Compliance Leads advise on applicable security, privacy, and regulatory requirements, helping teams identify controls and manage related risks. They provide guidance and review within their expertise; designated owners retain formal approval authority.

### Responsibilities
- Identify applicable security, privacy, and compliance requirements
- Advise on risk assessment, control design, and evidence needed for review
- Review proposed designs or release plans for relevant security and compliance concerns
- Escalate unresolved risks and clarify required sign-offs

### Goals
- Address security and compliance needs early in delivery
- Make ownership, evidence, and unresolved risks clear
- Reduce the likelihood of preventable security or compliance gaps

### Typical Communication
- Work with Developers on secure design and implementation practices
- Coordinate validation and evidence with QA Leads
- Align required reviews and approvals with Release Managers
- Surface risks, dependencies, and decisions to Project Managers and relevant stakeholders

---

## Customer / Support Representatives

### Role Summary
Customer or Support Representatives bring customer-facing and operational support perspectives into planning and delivery, and help prepare support teams for changes.

### Responsibilities
- Share customer feedback, common support issues, and operational needs
- Identify supportability, documentation, and training requirements
- Review customer-facing changes and support materials for clarity
- Help prepare support teams for launch and collect post-release feedback

### Goals
- Ensure delivered changes address customer needs and can be supported effectively
- Reduce avoidable customer confusion and support burden
- Close the feedback loop between customers, support, and product teams

### Typical Communication
- Share customer insights and adoption concerns with Product Managers
- Work with Developers on supportability and operational handoffs
- Coordinate realistic support scenarios and validation with QA Leads
- Align launch readiness, support materials, and communications with Release Managers
